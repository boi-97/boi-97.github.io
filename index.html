Boyu Mu

PhD Candidate in Economics, Cardiff University

Economics PhD researcher combining econometric methods with multimodal machine learning to study housing markets and spatial inequality. My work draws on images, text and geospatial data to measure neighbourhood and housing characteristics that are difficult to capture with conventional data, with an emphasis on interpretable models that speak to policy-relevant questions. I bring an interdisciplinary background spanning business, education and finance, together with practical experience in financial research and international academic exchange.
